MISSION 003 // PUZZLE PLATFORMER

FLIPSIDE

A gravity-switch puzzle-platformer concept where the player changes orientation to turn ceilings into floors and discover a route through a layered escape-room environment.

CONCEPT BRIEF

THE MECHANIC

Gravity is the central puzzle mechanic. The player identifies valid shift points, targets a location, and flips gravity to move between surfaces that would otherwise be ceilings or walls.

DESIGN FOCUS

  • Gravity-shift navigation.
  • Spatial reasoning and route planning.
  • Room-scale puzzle progression.
  • Clear feedback for valid shift points and orientation changes.
