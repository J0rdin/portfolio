MISSION 002 // GAME CONCEPT

BIKE RIDE SIMULATOR

A cooperative bike journey concept centered on the ride itself: choose a bike and destination, explore routes with friends, take photographs, collect souvenirs, and enjoy the journey.

REPLACE WITH YOUR BIKE / ENVIRONMENT ART⌁ 🚲 ⌁

Suggested media: bike selection, scenic road, travel UI, map, or photography mechanic.

CONCEPT BRIEF

THE EXPERIENCE

The concept focuses on a relaxed, exploratory ride with co-op play. Players can select a bike and destination, travel through environments, take pictures of places and rare finds, and collect souvenirs along the route.

CORE LOOP

  • Choose a bike and destination.
  • Ride and explore the route.
  • Take photographs of scenery and discoveries.
  • Collect souvenirs and share the journey with friends.
