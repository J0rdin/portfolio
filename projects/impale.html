MISSION 001 // GAMEPLAY PROTOTYPE

IMPALE

A first-person platforming experience set above a sea of molten lava. Players navigate floating platforms while falling nails destroy paths beneath them, then activate two objectives before reaching the final portal.

MISSION BRIEF

THE OBJECTIVE

Survive the hazardous environment, complete Objective A and Objective B in sequence, and reach the final destination while navigating collapsing pathways and avoiding the nail storm.

CORE SYSTEMS

  • First-person platforming and precision jumping.
  • Falling nail hazards that destroy platforms on impact.
  • Checkpoint and respawn system.
  • Sequential objectives and level progression.
  • Environmental hazards and UI notifications.

DEVELOPMENT NOTES

Developed in Unreal Engine 5.6 using Blueprint scripting. The documented systems include hazard spawning and collision detection, dynamic platform destruction, objective tracking, checkpoint handling, respawning, and game-state/UI management.
